MXene Auto Manager quota policy: verified-unreserved-fixed-threshold-v1

Single source of truth: final_configuration.quota_eligibility.
Original 8-job/40-PUB Runtime Batch: at least 150.0 verified available seconds.
Targeted PUB / missing-treatment / grouped recovery Batch: at least 40.0 seconds.
Thresholds are inclusive. 155 seconds qualifies for an original Batch and 45
seconds qualifies for a replacement even when estimate plus margin is larger.

Available = provider remaining minus uncharged local reservations, capped by
explicit authorized per-account headroom. Provider usage must be fresh, complete,
consistent and scoped to the authorized instance. Never subtract already charged
completed usage a second time. Pending/UNKNOWN jobs require reconciliation; they
are not silently treated as free capacity. Provider exhaustion is authoritative.

New quota reservations use the admission threshold, not a runtime prediction:
150 total split across original Batch jobs, 40 for one PUB replacement, or one
40-second reservation shared across an explicitly grouped recovery. Existing
reservations are immutable historical accounting until settled/released through
the normal lifecycle; no migration or retrospective resizing is performed.
Atomic SQLite reservation checks and WorkerLock prevent concurrent over-allocation.
These tokens are admission accounting, not a promise of actual runtime: the IBM
provider enforces its own quota and actual usage is reconciled normally.

Runtime estimates, evidence confidence, and safety_margin_seconds remain recorded
for diagnostics, warnings and provenance. The runtime-plus-margin value is not
compared against quota for admission. required_quota_seconds is authoritative;
required_runtime_with_margin_seconds is telemetry. The legacy output key
required_seconds_including_margin aliases required_quota_seconds for compatibility.

No change to account authorization/priorities, frozen inputs, backend/instance
identity, PUB scope, UNKNOWN safeguards, duplicate prevention or accepted results.
Explicit per-account authorization limits still apply. When provider quota or
unreserved headroom is below threshold, select another authorized eligible account
or wait with bounded backoff; do not repeatedly call submission on an ambiguous ID.
Targeted replacement attempts remain unlimited and only missing/invalid slots qualify.

final_config.json retains safety_margin_seconds as diagnostic metadata. Its schema
and scientific settings are unchanged. Normalized configuration exposes quota_policy,
runtime_estimate_and_margin_mode and the two centralized minimum thresholds.
No live IBM jobs may be submitted by offline tests.
